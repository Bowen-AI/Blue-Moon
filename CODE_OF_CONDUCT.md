# Code Of Conduct

Blue Moon should be a welcoming project for people who want to help communities do real-world good.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and specific.
- Welcome people with different levels of technical experience.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Keep safety, privacy, and accessibility concerns visible.
- Give organizers, participants, and local communities the benefit of thoughtful design.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or behavior.
- Sexualized comments or unwanted attention.
- Sharing private information without permission.
- Spam, scams, or attempts to misuse the project for unsafe events.

## Reporting

If something feels unsafe or hostile, open a GitHub issue if it is appropriate to discuss publicly. For sensitive reports, contact the project owner privately through GitHub.

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or block contributors who make the project unsafe for others.
