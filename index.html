What it is

The traction test

Make doing one good thing feel social, easy, and worth sharing.

Blue Moon starts as a lightweight place to organize cleanups, community help, and local volunteering. The first version cares about a single loop: see a good action, join or create one, do it, post proof, inspire the next person.

Proof loop

Completed events should feel good to share.

  1. See a clear local action.
  2. Join with low friction.
  3. Do the real-world work.
  4. Post proof with photos and impact.
  5. Share the card so another person starts.

Organizations

Create an org, then approve it once.

Organizations get a lightweight approval step before they can post under an official name. One-off community activities do not need approval.

Create and join

Post an activity for people to browse and join.

One-off posts go live right away. Approved organizations can post under their org name after the org approval step above.
